## Visual verdict

- The experience looks like Buffer: an evidence and confirmation surface, not ChatGPT.
- No assistant avatar, message bubbles, greeting, personality or conversation history appears.
- Amount, date, item type and Reality/Scenario distinction can be checked at a glance.
- Desktop uses the adjacent capture panel; mobile keeps a focused, safe-area-aware flow without horizontal overflow.
- Automated acceptance also samples all six skins and proves distinct capture-surface styling without changing semantics or controls.
